# OmniBar Privacy Policy for Firefox

OmniBar has no servers, accounts, analytics, or ads. The developer does not receive your data.

## Data on your device

OmniBar stores your app list and order, cached icons, default app, app opening preference, theme, and page access preference in your browser. Uninstalling OmniBar removes its browser storage. App exports and data held by websites are managed separately.

## Tab access and the clipboard

When you allow optional tab access, OmniBar reads the active tab’s address and title to show the current page in the dock. The page actions copy the address or a prompt containing it to your clipboard. Paste it into the app of your choice. OmniBar keeps no history of tab addresses or titles. Turn page access off in OmniBar Settings or revoke it in Firefox’s extension permissions.

## External services

Google’s favicon service receives app hostnames and the requested icon size when OmniBar loads or refreshes an icon. Your browser sends requests directly to the websites you open. Each site handles your interactions and any prompts you paste under its own privacy policy.

OmniBar requests access to its default app sites and can ask for access to individual custom sites. It uses this access to display its dock controls only on the native sidebar pages it opens. OmniBar does not read app content, conversations, or sign-in cookies.
